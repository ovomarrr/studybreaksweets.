our story

Small business.
Big cookie plans.

Baked by Piper is a student-run cookie business made for students who want an easy, affordable treat during the school day.

We serve Woodbridge High School, Lake Braddock Secondary School (LBSS), and Edison High School. Everything is designed to be simple: pick your cookies, choose your school, and place your order.

questions?

Good to know.

Where do you sell?

We currently serve Woodbridge High School, Lake Braddock Secondary School (LBSS), and Edison High School.

How do I order?

Use the Order page, choose your school and pickup details, add your cookies, and submit the form.

When can I pick up my order?

Choose an available date and time in the order form. Edit your actual pickup schedule in your business information before publishing.

How much do cookies cost?

Prices shown on the menu are sample prices. You can update every price in config.js.

Can I order multiple cookies?

Yes! Use the + and − controls next to each cookie to build one order with multiple products.

How far in advance should I order?

Set your own ordering deadline based on your baking and school schedule. This answer is editable.
